Questions people ask

When can I download Plyndi?

Not yet. It’s a native iPhone app in active development, and App Store submission is the next milestone. There’s no fixed date yet — join the launch list and you’ll get one email the day it’s live. The build log shows exactly what’s finished.

How much will it cost?

Plyndi Premium is the subscription that unlocks the AI features. The price isn’t final yet; it will be published here before launch, and billing goes through Apple.

Is my money data private?

Plyndi never connects to your bank — you enter amounts yourself. By default your data stays on your device. When you use an AI feature, only the data that feature needs (for example, recent expenses for a spending summary) is sent to Plyndi’s server and an AI provider. No ads, no data sale. Details in the app privacy policy.

Do I need an account?

You sign in with Apple or Google; Plyndi only receives the basic profile those providers share. You can delete your profile and data from Settings at any time.

Will there be an Android version?

It’s planned, on the same backend, after the iPhone launch. If you use Android, pick it on the launch-list form — that tells me how many people are waiting.

Is the trip sketch on this page AI?

No, and it doesn’t pretend to be. It arranges a fixed list of well-known anchor activities across the number of days you enter. Treat it as a frame to rearrange, then read the full guide for hours, prices and how to get between stops.

How does Plyndi make money?

Plyndi is funded by Plyndi Premium subscriptions and by client projects (website and app development). The travel guides may carry affiliate links on some booking buttons — a temporary way to keep them free, at no extra cost to you. There is no display advertising on this site. Details on the disclosure page.

How often are guides updated?

Each guide shows a “last updated” date. Prices, opening hours and transit details change, so guides get revisited rather than left to go stale — and corrections from readers get handled first.
