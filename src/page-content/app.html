What Plyndi does

One app instead of a notes file, a spreadsheet, a budget tracker, a workout log and three booking confirmations in your inbox.

Daily Briefing

What today holds — tasks, calendar and weather — in one screen you can read in ten seconds. The habit the whole app is built around.

Money

Budgets and expenses in any currency, converted the moment you log them, plus an AI analyst that tells you what’s actually happening with your money.

Fitness

AI workout plans tuned to your week, with camera form check while you train.

Travel

Day-by-day itineraries with transit legs between stops, trip budgets that stay honest across currencies, and packing lists tied to the trip.

Quick Add

Type or dictate one sentence — “dinner 480 baht in Bangkok” — and it files itself as an expense, a task or a packing item. Receipts scan from the camera.

🌎

Built for Asia

Multiple languages, multiple currencies, and guides written from Taipei rather than translated from a US template.
