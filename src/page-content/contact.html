Get in touch

Corrections, destination suggestions, partnership or advertising inquiries — all welcome.

Sent via Formspree — it'll land directly in your inbox.

Other ways to reach me

If the form doesn’t work on your device, email directly:

  • contact@plyndi.com — guides, corrections, suggestions, partnerships.
  • support@plyndi.com — anything to do with the Plyndi app, or a request about your data. The support page lists what to include.

Plyndi is run by one person alongside university, so replies usually take one to three days.

Before you write in

  • Spotted outdated info in a guide? Include the page and what changed — corrections get prioritized.
  • Suggesting a destination? Tell me why it's worth covering, not just the name — it helps with research.
  • Partnership inquiries: include your company, what you're proposing, and relevant links.
  • App bug? Include your device, iOS version and the app version from Settings — it saves a round trip.
