App Development

Custom iOS, Android and web apps — from a focused MVP to an AI-powered tool your customers or team use every day.

Start an app project → What we build

What we build

Every app is planned around what your business and your users actually need. Start with the essentials and add more once it’s live.

iOS apps

Native iPhone and iPad apps, built to Apple’s guidelines and prepared for App Store review.

Android apps

Apps for Android phones and tablets, prepared for publishing on Google Play.

Cross-platform apps

One shared codebase for iOS and Android when that suits your budget and timeline better than two separate apps.

Web apps

Apps that run in the browser on any device — customer portals, booking tools, internal tools — with nothing to install.

AI features

Practical AI where it helps: drafting, summarising, search, chat assistants and smart suggestions, set up around your own content and rules.

Admin dashboards

A web dashboard for your team to manage users, content, orders or bookings and see what’s happening in the app.

Backend & APIs

Databases, user accounts and logins, payments and subscriptions, push notifications, and connections to the services you already use.

How we work

  1. DiscoveryWe talk through your idea, your users and the must-have features. You fill in a private project form so nothing gets missed.
  2. DesignUser flows and screen designs for the key parts of the app, agreed with you before building starts.
  3. BuildThe app, its backend and the admin dashboard are built in stages, with progress shared as we go.
  4. TestTesting on real devices and browsers, plus a round of your own testing before launch.
  5. LaunchWe help you publish to the App Store and Google Play, or put your web app live.
  6. SupportFixes, updates and new features after launch, if you’d like ongoing maintenance.

We agree on scope, timeline and price with you before any work starts.

What to prepare

You don’t need all of this to get in touch — but the more you have, the faster we can give you a clear plan.

  • A short description of the idea and the problem it solves
  • Who will use the app — customers, your team, or both
  • Your must-have features, and the nice-to-haves
  • Apps you like (or don’t) and why
  • Your logo, brand colours and fonts, if you have them
  • Any sketches, wireframes or screenshots
  • An existing app, website or data we should know about
  • A rough budget range and a target launch date
  • Apple Developer and Google Play accounts — or let us know if you need help setting them up
