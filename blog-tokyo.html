First-Time Tokyo Itinerary & Transit Tips

Updated July 2026  |  8 Min Read  |  By Plyndi Editorial Team

Tokyo is a thrilling metropolis where futuristic skyscrapers and glowing neon districts stand side-by-side with tranquil Shinto shrines and historic gardens.

1. Mastering Public Transit: IC Cards

Tokyo's rail network is fast, clean, and punctual. Add a digital Suica or Pasmo card directly to your smartphone's wallet app to tap in and out of subways, trains, and convenience stores instantly without buying paper tickets.

Essential Tip:

Rent a Pocket Wi-Fi or activate an eSIM before leaving the airport. Google Maps navigation is vital for maneuvering through complex stations like Shinjuku.

2. Iconic Districts: Shibuya Crossing & Shinjuku

Experience the energy of Shibuya Crossing—the busiest pedestrian intersection in the world. Afterward, take the JR Yamanote line to Shinjuku for rooftop views at the Tokyo Metropolitan Government Building and dinner in the narrow lanes of Omoide Yokocho.

3. Traditional Tokyo: Asakusa & Senso-ji Temple

Step back in time by visiting Asakusa, home to Senso-ji—Tokyo’s oldest Buddhist temple. Walk through the historic Nakamise Shopping Street to sample traditional Japanese rice crackers and sweet matcha treats.
