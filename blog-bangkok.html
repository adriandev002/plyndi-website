The Ultimate First-Timer’s Guide to Bangkok

Updated September 2026 | Destination Guides

Bangkok is a vibrant metropolis combining ancient traditions with modern urban life. Whether you're visiting for temples, street food, or shopping, this guide covers the essentials.

1. Top Attractions

Visit the Grand Palace, Wat Pho (Reclining Buddha), and Wat Arun. Take a ferry ride on the Chao Phraya River for an affordable and scenic transport experience.

2. Street Food Hotspots

Head to Yaowarat Road (Chinatown) at night for world-famous Pad Thai, fresh seafood, and Mango Sticky Rice.

3. Getting Around

Use the BTS Skytrain and MRT Underground systems to bypass Bangkok’s notorious traffic jams easily.
