Bali Budget Travel Hacks & Hidden Beaches

Updated August 2026  |  7 Min Read  |  By Plyndi Editorial Team

Bali remains one of the world's premier tropical destinations. While luxury resorts dominate Uluwatu and Seminyak, exploring the Island of the Gods on a budget is surprisingly simple with the right local insights.

1. Transport: Scooter Rentals & Gojek Hacks

Taxis in tourist zones like Canggu can be expensive due to local transport monopolies. To save money, rent an automatic scooter for around $5–$7 USD per day, or use ride-hailing apps like Gojek and Grab for cheap motorcycle taxi transfers.

Insider Money Tip:

Dine at authentic local eateries called Warungs. Ordering Nasi Goreng or Nasi Campur costs around $1.50 to $3.00 USD compared to $12+ at Western cafes.

2. Escape the Crowds: Hidden Beaches in South Kuta

Skip overcrowded Kuta and Seminyak beaches. Head south toward Green Bowl Beach, Thomas Beach, or Melasti Beach to enjoy limestone cliffs, turquoise waves, and quiet coastlines with minimal entry fees.

3. Cultural Exploration: Ubud & Waterfalls

Base yourself in Ubud for a few days to explore the Tegallalang Rice Terraces, the Sacred Monkey Forest Sanctuary, and stunning jungle waterfalls like Tegenungan and Leke Leke.
