The First-Timer’s Guide to Paris: Landmarks & Insider Secrets

Updated September 2026  |  6 Min Read  |  By Plyndi Editorial Team

Paris, the City of Light, captivates world travelers with grand boulevards, world-class art collections, historic cafes, and unmistakable architectural landmarks. Here is everything you need to know for your first trip to France's iconic capital.

1. Iconic Views: Eiffel Tower & Seine River Cruises

No trip to Paris is complete without witnessing the Eiffel Tower. For the most dramatic photo angles, head to the Trocadéro Plaza or enjoy a picnic on the lawns of Champ de Mars. At night, board an evening Seine River Cruise to watch Paris illuminate under the stars.

Pro Traveler Tip:

Use the Paris Metro (RATP) network instead of taxis. Purchasing a Navigo Easy pass or multi-day travel pass saves both money and time in heavy traffic.

2. World-Class Art: Louvre & Musée d'Orsay

The Louvre Museum houses tens of thousands of historic artworks, including the Mona Lisa and Venus de Milo. If you prefer Impressionist masterpieces, walk across the Seine to the stunning Musée d'Orsay, housed in a former Beaux-Arts railway station.

3. Charming Neighborhoods: Montmartre & Le Marais

Spend an afternoon strolling through the winding hilltop streets of Montmartre up to the breathtaking Sacré-Cœur Basilica for panoramic views of Paris. Later, explore Le Marais for trendy boutiques, historical courtyards, and famous bakeries serving fresh croissants.
