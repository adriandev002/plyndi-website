Plyndi, the app

The guides tell you what a trip costs. The app is where you actually plan it — day-by-day itineraries with transit legs, a trip budget that handles paying in three currencies in one week, and packing lists tied to the trip.

  • Log an expense in the currency you paid in; it converts on entry, so the trip total stays honest.
  • Add anything in one sentence — typed or dictated — and it files itself.
  • Draft an itinerary with AI, then rearrange it yourself. A starting point, not a cage.

It’s not on the App Store yet. The app page lists exactly what’s built and what isn’t.

Questions people ask

Is the Plyndi app available yet?

Not yet. It’s a native iOS app in active development — itineraries, trip budgets and packing lists work; App Store submission is the next milestone. The app page lists what’s finished and what isn’t, and you can join the launch list there.

Is the trip skeleton generator on this page AI?

No, and it doesn’t pretend to be. It arranges a fixed list of well-known anchor activities across the number of days you enter. Treat it as a frame to rearrange, then read the full guide for hours, prices and how to get between stops. The AI planner is a feature of the app, not this page.

How does Plyndi make money?

Affiliate commissions on bookings made through partner links, at no extra cost to you. Plyndi is approved with Trip.com and has applied to Booking.com. There is no display advertising on this site. Details on the disclosure page.

How often are guides updated?

Each guide shows a “last updated” date. Prices, opening hours and transit details change, so guides get revisited rather than left to go stale — and corrections from readers get handled first.

Can I suggest a destination?

Yes — get in touch. New guides are added one at a time as they’re properly researched, so it may take a while, but suggestions genuinely shape what’s covered next.
