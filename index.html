Questions people ask

How does the trip skeleton generator work?

It's a simple template tool, not artificial intelligence — it arranges a short list of well-known anchor activities across the number of days you enter, based on the pace you pick. Treat it as a starting frame to rearrange, and read the full destination guide for the details (hours, prices, how to get between stops).

Is Plyndi affiliated with any booking platforms?

Plyndi participates in affiliate programs with select travel booking platforms, meaning we may earn a small commission if you book through a link on this site — at no extra cost to you. See the affiliate disclosure page for the full list and policy.

How often are guides updated?

Each guide shows a "last updated" date. Prices, opening hours, and transit details change, so guides are revisited periodically rather than left to go stale.

Can I suggest a destination?

Yes — get in touch. New guides are added one at a time as they're properly researched, so it may take a while, but suggestions genuinely shape what's covered next.
