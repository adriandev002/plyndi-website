Top 5 Hidden Spots in Myanmar

Published: September 2026 | Category: Travel Guides

Myanmar is famous for Bagan and Inle Lake, but there are numerous untouched gems waiting for adventurous travelers. Here are five lesser-known destinations to include in your trip itinerary.

1. Hpa-An (Kayin State)

Surrounded by dramatic limestone mountains and paddy fields, Hpa-An is a haven for cave explorers and hikers.

2. Mergui Archipelago

Located in southern Myanmar, this region features over 800 tropical islands with clear waters and world-class diving.

3. Kyauk Kalap Pagoda

A striking rock formation topped with a Buddhist temple, surrounded by a serene artificial lake in Hpa-An.
